Step 1: Add Your Options

Open the decision wheel generator and clear any default entries with the Clear All button. Type each choice into the options field one at a time, or paste a full list at once using bulk import — up to 20 options are supported, which is plenty for most decisions.

Step 2: Style the Wheel

Switch to the Palette tab to choose a color scheme, or let each slice cycle through the default rainbow gradient automatically. If a decision benefits from visuals — like choosing a vacation spot — upload a small image to a slice from the Options tab so it's instantly recognizable mid-spin.

Use Sort A-Z to tidy a long list, or Shuffle if you want the slice order itself to feel more random before you even spin.

Step 3: Spin and Decide

Press the SPIN button and let physics take over. The wheel gradually slows under simulated friction until the pointer lands on a single slice — your decision, made without bias. Toggle Winner Removal if you want to work through a list of choices one at a time rather than allowing repeats.

Quick tipEnable Sound On for a satisfying ticking effect as the wheel slows — it makes even a small decision feel like an event.
