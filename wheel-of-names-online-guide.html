Why Use a Name Wheel?

An online wheel of names makes random selection quick and visible. Everyone can see the list, the spin, and the result, which helps groups trust the process.

Classroom Ideas

Use it for discussion leaders, reading turns, lab partners, exit-ticket shares, and reward choices. Remove a name after selection for an easy no-repeat mode.

Raffles and Teams

Paste in eligible entries for a small giveaway or use the wheel to divide players into teams. Check the list carefully before spinning and announce the rules first.

Quick tip Keep a backup copy of your names so you can repeat a draw transparently if a technical issue occurs.
