The 'What Should I Eat' Problem

Deciding what to eat is a small choice that somehow eats up a surprising amount of mental energy every single day. A food wheel removes the back-and-forth entirely — load in your options, spin, and cook or order whatever it lands on. It's decisive, it's fun, and nobody has to be "the one who picked."

Building Your Food Wheel

Open the free spin wheel tool and try the built-in food preset for a quick start, or build your own from your family's regular rotation. Group similar meals — pasta night, taco night, stir-fry — so every result feels like a real option nobody dreads.

Making It a Household Habit

Save your food wheel as a bookmark so it's one tap away every evening. Add a slice for "cook something new" occasionally to keep the rotation from getting stale, and let kids add their own favorite meals so everyone feels involved in the decision.

Quick tipAdd photos of each dish to the slices — a visual food wheel is more fun to spin and easier to decide from at a glance.
