Festive Prompt Ideas

Add holiday songs, trivia categories, gift-wrapping challenges, cocoa toppings, family activities, and short kindness tasks. Use a separate prize wheel if you are hosting a larger event.

Make It Work for All Ages

Mix movement, creativity, and conversation so every guest has options. Keep prompts short, safe, and optional, especially when children and adults play together.

Hosting Tips

Test the wheel before guests arrive, use large text on a shared screen, and prepare a “skip” slice. A saved wheel makes annual events easier to run.

Quick tip Let guests add one prompt each before the first spin to create a wheel that feels personal.
