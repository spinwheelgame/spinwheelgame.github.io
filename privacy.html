🔒 Your Data Safety

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2, 2026

Welcome to Spin the Wheel Game (https://spinwheelgame.github.io/spin-wheel-game). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal information we receive and collect when you use our website, as well as how we safeguard and manage your information.

1. Information We Collect and Store

We believe in data minimization. Our decision-maker tool is designed to process your inputs locally whenever possible:

  • Local Browser Storage: Custom options, names, color themes, and game lists you enter on the wheel are stored locally in your web browser (via LocalStorage). This data remains on your device and is not sent to or saved on our servers.
  • Log Files: Like most standard website servers, we utilize log files. The information inside log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks to analyze trends, administer the site, track user movement, and gather demographic information. IP addresses and other such information are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

2. Cookies, Google AdSense, and Third-Party Advertising

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

  • Google AdSense & DoubleClick Cookies: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opting Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected via standard web analytics and log files is used solely to maintain, optimize, and secure our website performance, analyze traffic patterns, detect potential technical issues, and improve user experience across different devices.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. Please be aware that we are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such external sites. We encourage our users to read the privacy statements of every website that collects personally identifiable information.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Protecting children's privacy online is especially important. Spin the Wheel Game does not knowingly collect or solicit any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately so we can remove such information from our records.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of our website following any changes signifies your acceptance of the updated policy.
