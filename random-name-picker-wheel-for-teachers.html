Why Random Selection Matters in the Classroom

Students notice when the same few classmates get called on repeatedly, and it quietly discourages quieter students from raising their hands at all. A random name picker puts every student on equal footing — the wheel doesn't know who's shy, who's confident, or who answered last time.

Setting Up a Name Picker Wheel

Paste your class list into the spin wheel tool, and use Sort A-Z to keep the list organized between updates as students join or leave the roster. Turning on Winner Removal converts the tool into a full-rotation picker: once everyone's been selected, clear and reload the full list to start again.

When to Use It

  • Cold-calling during discussion-based lessons
  • Choosing who reads the next paragraph aloud
  • Assigning presentation order for group projects
  • Picking a line leader or classroom helper for the day
  • Selecting volunteers for a demonstration

Keeping It Engaging, Not Stressful

Frame the wheel as a game rather than a test — a quick spin before a question softens the moment and gives students a second to prepare. For anxious students, consider a rule where a lucky re-spin token can be earned or given occasionally, keeping the tool fair while staying kind.
