How to Play

Fill the white squares with letters to form words that match the clues.

1. Tap any white cell to select it. The clue for that word appears above the grid.

2. Use the on-screen or physical keyboard to type letters.

3. Tap the same cell again (or press the arrow keys) to switch between Across and Down.

4. Numbers in the corner tell you which clue starts there.

5. Use Check to see which letters are correct, Reveal for a hint, or Clear to erase your entries.
