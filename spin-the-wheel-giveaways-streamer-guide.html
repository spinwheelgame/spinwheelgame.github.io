Why Streamers Love a Spin Wheel

Viewers tune in for moments, and a spin the wheel giveaway creates one on demand. It's visual, it's suspenseful, and unlike a random number generator buried in chat, everyone watching can see exactly how the winner was chosen — which builds trust in the result.

Running a Fair Giveaway Wheel

Paste your list of eligible viewer names into the wheel spinner using bulk import, then share your screen before spinning so chat sees the same list you're working from. Because the wheel logs a spin history, you can screenshot the result as proof for any post-stream questions about who won.

Beyond Giveaways: Challenge & Content Wheels

  • Challenge wheel — spin for the next in-game challenge or handicap.
  • Content wheel — let chat vote, then spin between the top suggestions for what to play next.
  • Donation goal wheel — spin a reward wheel each time a donation milestone is hit.
  • Icebreaker wheel — spin a question to answer during a just-chatting segment.

Keeping It Smooth On Screen

Turn off sound if your stream already has background music, and keep the options list short and readable — six to twelve slices display best at stream resolution. A capture-friendly layout means viewers can follow along even from a phone screen.

Quick tipRe-spin between segments rather than leaving the same wheel up the whole stream; a fresh option list keeps the moment feeling special.
