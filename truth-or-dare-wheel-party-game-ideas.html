Why a Wheel Beats a Traditional Truth or Dare

Classic truth or dare relies on someone making up a prompt on the spot, which often means the same handful of dares get reused all night. A truth or dare wheel replaces that guesswork with a curated list you control, so every spin lands on something genuinely fun.

Building Two Wheels: Truths and Dares

Set up one spin wheel for truth prompts and a second for dares, and let players spin a coin flip or simple yes/no wheel first to decide which list they're pulling from. This two-wheel approach keeps categories clean and easy to browse.

Prompt Ideas by Age Group

Family-Friendly (all ages)

Sing a song for 15 seconds, do your best animal impression, share your most embarrassing childhood story, do 10 jumping jacks, tell a joke.

Teens

Text a friend a silly message they dictate, do an impression of another player, answer a lighthearted personal question, attempt a TikTok dance trend.

Adults

Share an embarrassing work story, answer a relationship-related truth question, do a themed dare tied to the party's occasion, swap seats with another player for a round.

Keeping the Game Comfortable for Everyone

Add a "skip" slice to each wheel so nobody feels cornered by a prompt they're not comfortable with, and keep dares physically safe and inclusive of guests who may not want to be the center of attention for too long.

Quick tipColor-code the truth wheel and dare wheel differently so it's obvious at a glance which one is spinning.
